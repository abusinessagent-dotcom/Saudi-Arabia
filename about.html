About Saudi Return House

Over 14 years of connecting trusted drivers with Saudi families across the Kingdom. Built on integrity, backed by results.

Who We Are

A Trusted Name in Driver Placement

Saudi Return House is a premier driver recruitment and placement agency serving Saudi families since 2010. We were founded with a single mission: make it easy, safe, and reliable for Saudi families to find professional, verified household drivers.

Over the years we have built a strong network of qualified drivers from India, Pakistan, the Philippines, and other countries — all pre-screened, document-verified, and experienced in serving Saudi households with professionalism and respect.

What We Stand For

  • Providing Saudi families with 100% background-verified, professional drivers
  • Ensuring every driver has valid documentation, clean record, and proper licensing
  • Offering complete visa processing and air ticket support for seamless placement
  • Supporting drivers with fair, transparent job opportunities and legal employment
  • Maintaining long-term relationships built on trust, not just transactions
  • Delivering fast, responsive support via WhatsApp — 7 days a week
